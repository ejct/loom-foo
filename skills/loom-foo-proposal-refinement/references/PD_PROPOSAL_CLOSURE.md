# Proposal Closure — conditional methodology

> Applies only when the owning `SKILL.md` conditional index selects it. Moved from the v0.29.0 root. References mentioned as `references/X.md` inside quoted historical text are relative to the *owning skill directory*, not this file. General L1 authority and current use-first instructions prevail over superseded historical clauses.

## 13. Describe the conclusion proportionately

### `CONVERGED` (for substantial reviewed proposals)

Use only when applicable conditions hold:

- no unresolved CRITICAL findings;
- no unresolved MAJOR findings;
- no material assumption is presented as established fact;
- the proposal still satisfies the current Proposal Charter and Proposal Quality Contract;
- previous material fixes survive regression checking;
- important claims have a credible validation path;
- remaining uncertainty is bounded, explicitly accepted, or no longer resolvable by more reasoning;
- another audit pass has low expected information value.

### `VALIDATION_REQUIRED`

Use only when a specific load-bearing empirical uncertainty requires a test before a consequential decision. Otherwise bound it and continue authorized safe work.

Return the smallest useful validation step and what result would change the decision.

### `BLOCKED`

Use when a missing fact, authority decision, dependency, constraint, credential, or other prerequisite prevents a defensible proposal or a material revision.

State the exact blocker and the minimum input/action needed.

### `NON_CONVERGING`

Use when repeated revisions keep producing material findings without a declining trend.

Identify the unstable framing, requirement, assumption, or architecture that should be reopened instead of continuing mechanically.
## 14. Stop before checking becomes ceremony

Stop once the proposal addresses the owner objective, material findings have a disposition, uncertainty is honestly bounded and another check is unlikely to change the next choice. Never add a dedicated validation phase, benchmark or independent panel solely to make the proposal look rigorous. If a real high-consequence risk remains, disclose it and select the smallest necessary check. Continuing an already authorized project is the default where safe and useful.
## 15. Final output

Return the latest proposal as the primary artifact.

For consequential reviewed proposals, briefly state material corrections, remaining risks and the actual next step. Formal Audit Closure, pass counts, version lineage and full finding registers are optional and only useful when required by the accepted artifact or reviewer contract. Do not dump an intermediate audit transcript.
## Final checks

Before finishing, verify that:

- the final proposal addresses the reconstructed problem rather than only the first implementation idea;
- the requested decision and proposed delta are reconstructable by the intended reader;
- a simpler credible baseline and real alternatives were considered where material;
- when external prior art could change a material choice, its effect on the initial solution space was reconciled; no unnecessary discovery step was imposed;
- accepted project decisions and authority boundaries were respected or explicitly reopened;
- facts, assumptions, interpretations, and unresolved questions are distinguishable;
- material irreversibility/lock-in and revisit conditions are explicit when applicable;
- every **identified** CRITICAL and MAJOR finding has an explicit disposition, without inventing findings or a registry;
- resolved findings stayed resolved after later revisions;
- completion/convergence is bounded to actual evidence and is not claimed solely because the reviewer stopped finding issues;
- empirical uncertainties are bounded or handed to the responsible owner only where they genuinely affect the next decision;
- a repeated review, if any, stopped for a substantive reason rather than a required pass count.
