# Identity Design

## Result contract

Turn an approved brand brief and constraints into a recognizable, coherent **visual identity system**, with actual files/rules at the fidelity requested. A logo preview alone is not a full identity. Do not assert a mark is legally unique, registrable, protected or commercially cleared without a proper legal/trademark process.

## Own this / adjacent owners

Use for new or revised visual identities, brand marks/wordmarks, coherent signature systems, symbol/color/type rules, identity applications, icon-of-brand integration and identity guidelines.

- One product icon/app launcher or family of action glyphs -> `loom-foo-icon-design` unless tied to larger identity strategy.
- Typography craft or detailed type system -> `loom-foo-typography`; identity owns approved brand voice/role and brand-wide constraints.
- Standalone poster/campaign visual -> `loom-foo-graphic-design` following any supplied identity.
- Token/component design-system across products -> `loom-foo-design-systems` for system semantics, after identity decisions.
- Review-only brand critique -> `loom-foo-design-review`.
- Brand copywriting/narrative architecture -> Writing specialists; don't invent positioning research or audience quotes.

## Procedure

1. **Clarify identity subject.** Brand/product, audience, desired attributes, competitive/category references, language/scripts, existing marks, legal constraints, touchpoints and required deliverables. Distinguish existing accepted direction from open design space.
2. **Develop a coherent visual proposition.** Define visual principles that are specific enough to distinguish alternatives (e.g. geometry, visual rhythm, contrast, symbolism, material and typographic voice), not a generic 'minimal, modern, bold' brief. Read `references/IDENTITY_CONCEPT_AND_SYSTEM.md`.
3. **Design marks and system.** Construct a usable family: mark/wordmark/lockups, monochrome and reversed variants, typographic/color roles, compositions and applications within requested scope. Consider Ukrainian/Latin names and glyph coverage. Do not redraw third-party logos as original marks.
4. **Produce real assets.** Use requested vector/native sources and controlled export; include guidelines only when requested. Use `references/IDENTITY_DELIVERY_AND_QA.md` for optical and production-specific details. If only an image concept exists, call it a concept.
5. **Validate across touchpoints.** Inspect actual sizes, negative-space collapse, one-color/reversed use, locale, coexisting UI graphics and backgrounds, consistency between lockups and recognizability of primary mark. Separate optical judgment from objective file errors.
6. **Deliver and stop.** Provide assets or a scoped identity decision and rationale, source fidelity, outstanding rights/production checks. Do not imply trademark clearance, finished brand strategy, font licenses or full campaign was obtained unless actually checked.

## Internal QA

Check identity coherence and real use cases rather than just one attractive mockup. Separate visual distinctiveness from legal clearance. Optional standalone Design Review for consequential external critique; no compulsory review stage for a tiny export fix.

## Examples

- “Створи цілісну двомовну айдентику продукту” -> Identity Design, actual mark/usage family where requested.
- “Зроби набір стандартних toolbar icons” -> Icon Design.
- “Підбери шрифтові пари для наявного бренд-гіда” -> Typography unless new brand-system decisions are requested.
- “Порівняй два готові логотипи, без змін” -> Design Review.
