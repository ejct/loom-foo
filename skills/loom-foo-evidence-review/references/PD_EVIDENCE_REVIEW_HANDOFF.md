## 17. Decision boundary

Evidence review may conclude what the evidence supports, contradicts, or cannot establish **within scope**.

It must not choose the final project action. A downstream decision may legitimately incorporate:
- the Evidence Assessment;
- cost/opportunity cost;
- risk tolerance;
- reversibility;
- values/product priorities;
- authority constraints;
- timing.

That is outside this skill.
