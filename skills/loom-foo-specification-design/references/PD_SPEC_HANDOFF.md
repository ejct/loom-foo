# Spec Handoff — conditional methodology

> Applies only when the owning `SKILL.md` conditional index selects it. Moved from the v0.29.0 root. References mentioned as `references/X.md` inside quoted historical text are relative to the *owning skill directory*, not this file. General L1 authority and current use-first instructions prevail over superseded historical clauses.

## 15. Composition boundaries


- `loom-foo-proposal-refinement` may hand accepted/bounded intent to this skill.


- `loom-foo-architecture-design` consumes Specification constraints and may return newly discovered legitimate feasibility/compatibility constraints for explicit reconsideration; it must not silently rewrite desired semantics.


- `loom-foo-research-design` owns evidence-method design when a semantic requirement depends on empirical uncertainty.


- `loom-foo-work-decomposition` may consume the Specification to define implementation work without changing semantics.


- `loom-foo-agent-task-design` may project a bounded subset of the Specification into one executable Task Brief.


- future `loom-foo-change-verification` should compare an exact candidate with the accepted Specification rather than reconstructing intent from implementation.


- future project-knowledge stewardship may integrate accepted Specification state and downstream trace links.


Avoid recursive ownership.
## 16. Candidate validation requirements — use-first alignment (2026-11-17)

For ordinary compatible skill-content maintenance, verify frontmatter, required references, authority, semantic preservation and task-specific positive/negative examples; **use the revised skill in real work** rather than imposing a compulsory promotion campaign. The historical C0/no-skill comparison and unseen holdout requirements in the 0.4.0 root are explicitly superseded for this ordinary migration. If the user authorizes a frozen comparative campaign or a material empirical lift claim, retain a frozen subject, fair no-skill comparator, representative cases and a genuinely fresh holdout. Do not silently alter any already-frozen external evaluation plan.

Representative checks when material: low-risk simple behavior stays compact; accepted proposal does not rerun project-choice deliberation; `NEW_SPEC` versus `EVOLVE_SPEC` and `NO_SPEC_DELTA`; remove solution leakage while retaining mandated implementation constraints; handle authority conflicts, empirical uncertainty, missing failures/states, provenance and unaffected obligations; do not claim `SPEC_READY` without source fidelity. Architecture, implementation-task and conformance requests must route to their own owners. A static pass is never host-routing or model-application proof.

## Final checks


Before returning `SPEC_READY`, verify that:


- the controlling intent is authoritative enough for specification;


- the subject/baseline is clear;


- material obligations are independently referenceable;


- the contract says what must hold without unnecessary architecture;


- required states, transitions, failures and boundaries are covered when applicable;


- acceptance is conceptually decidable;


- material ambiguities are resolved or surfaced;


- solution leakage and overconstraint were challenged;


- backward provenance is sufficient;


- no empty future traceability was invented;


- the artifact is proportional to the problem;


- downstream owners can consume it without needing hidden conversation reasoning.
