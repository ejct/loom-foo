## Ukrainian-specific review lenses (conditional)

- Orthography: joined/separated/hyphenation of words, prefixes and particles; proper nouns; capitalization; alternate forms.
- Morphology: cases including vocative, gender/number agreement, verb and numeral forms; preserve names and proper-name spellings where unverified.
- Syntax/punctuation: clause boundaries, direct speech, quotations, lists; do not alter authorial rhythm when punctuation is admissible.
- Lexis: contextual meanings, collocations, calques, paronyms, register; do not label every colloquial word a grammatical error.
- Stylistics: audience, genre, inclusive terminology if relevant, editorial policy and tone. A style preference is not a mandatory language norm.
- Mixed language: preserve English technical APIs, identifiers, brand names, quotations and code-switching unless user authorizes localization. Do not “correct” English segments using Ukrainian orthographic assumptions.

## Representative reasoning examples (not normative oracle unless independently checked)

- `Я незнаю` → `Я не знаю` is a straightforward local correction in ordinary Ukrainian prose.
- `Працюєм` in deliberately colloquial dialogue may be an authorial choice; report context rather than automatically normalizing to `працюємо`.
- `Олеже` and `Олегу` can be considered as vocative alternatives; use the current primary source/lexicographic entry if making a strict normative claim about a form.
- `пів години`, `вебсайт`, `онлайн-конференція`: do not declare source/paragraph verified merely because this profile lists examples; inspect the official standard when contested or specifically requested.
- `Ми приймаємо участь`: a lexical/editorial concern distinct from spelling. Distinguish a recommended collocation from a claimed statutory or normative requirement.
- `Це якась хуйня`: correct spelling plus marked register is not an instruction to censor an author's words.
- A sentence like `Я бачив людину з біноклем` is semantically ambiguous; no arbitrary rewrite should select who holds the binoculars.

## Applying evidence

In routine low-risk cases use language competence for obvious corrections without pretending that a source lookup occurred. When normative certainty matters, seek the actual standard text or dictionary entry. If inaccessible, mark the rule unverified and offer a bounded suggestion; do not invent chapter/paragraph numbers or fabricate a quotation. Separate domain-specific or project-owned terminology from public Ukrainian norm.

## Profile boundaries

This profile is a selectively loaded reference, not a separate skill, machine dictionary, spellchecker, grammar engine or proof of Ukrainian-language accuracy. It must not override explicit authorial voice or the user's requested limits. For other locales, use appropriate local references or acknowledge unsupported normative certainty rather than defaulting to Ukrainian/English rules.
