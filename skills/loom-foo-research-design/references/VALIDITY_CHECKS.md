# Validity Checks


Use only the checks capable of changing interpretation. This is not a mandatory checklist for every plan.


## Decision validity
- Is the study tied to a real decision or claim?
- Could a plausible result change it?
- Is current evidence already sufficient?
- Is there a cheaper credible check?


## Construct validity
- Does the chosen observation measure the target property?
- Are proxies standing in for the real outcome?
- Can the proxy improve while the real outcome stays unchanged?
- Are supported and unsupported claims separated?


## Internal validity
For comparative/causal work:
- Are treatment and comparator different only in the intended way?
- Are known nuisance factors blocked/controlled/randomized when needed?
- Is the unit of assignment/replication correct?
- Can contamination or carryover explain the result?
- Was the treatment actually delivered in every arm?


## External validity
- What population/context/environment is represented?
- What important contexts are absent?
- Does the intended decision require generalization beyond the tested scope?


## Measurement validity
- Is the instrument stable/repeatable enough?
- Is resolution sufficient to see a meaningful difference?
- Is calibration/bias/drift material?
- Is the scorer/judge itself validated for this use?
- Is observer/evaluator dependence material?


## Statistical / sampling validity
When applicable:
- Is sample size adequate for the intended distinction?
- Are repeated observations incorrectly treated as independent units?
- Are multiple comparisons or optional stopping material?
- Is a null result being mistaken for equivalence?


Use specialist methodology when these questions determine the conclusion.


## Temporal validity
- Could provider/runtime/data behavior have changed during the study?
- Is the result tied to a dated version/time window?
- Would a later decision require revalidation?


## Operational validity
- Did the actual execution match the planned design?
- Were cases excluded after outcomes were visible?
- Did environment, tool access, budget, or retries drift across conditions?
- Were failed/broken runs distinguishable from real behavioral failures?


## Gaming / Goodhart risk
- What is the easiest way to raise the measured score without improving the real objective?
- Can the subject infer the evaluator and optimize for it?
- Does process compliance substitute for actual outcome quality?


## Exploratory / confirmatory integrity
- Was the hypothesis/criterion chosen before outcomes were visible?
- Were adaptations recorded?
- Are exploratory findings labeled as such?
- Is confirmatory follow-up required before a durable decision?


## Stop rule
Stop adding validity machinery when additional controls cannot reasonably change the decision or claim boundary.
