## 9. Analyze uncertainty for decision sensitivity

Not every uncertainty justifies more research.

Ask when material:
- can the uncertainty change option viability or recommendation?
- is the decision reversible?
- what is the cost/risk of delay?
- is reducing uncertainty plausibly worth the cost/latency?
- can a bounded reversible choice proceed?

The Decision Surface may recommend PROCEED, DEFER, or EXPERIMENT_FIRST. That does not authorize research execution.

If new evidence is chosen by the authority, `loom-foo-research-design` owns the next Evidence Plan.

Read `references/UNCERTAINTY_AND_REVERSIBILITY.md` for consequential uncertainty/lock-in decisions.
## 10. Use switch conditions where they add value

Prefer explicit conditions that would change the recommendation over vague caveats.

Use numeric thresholds only when grounded in accepted criteria/evidence. Do not invent thresholds for presentation.
