# Uncertainty and Reversibility

Uncertainty matters when it can change viability, preference, timing, or downside exposure.

## Decision sensitivity

Ask:
- Could this uncertainty invert the recommendation?
- Is the decision reversible?
- What is the downside if wrong?
- What is the cost of delay?
- Would additional evidence plausibly be worth the acquisition cost?
- Can a reversible bounded option buy information safely?

## Switch conditions

Prefer explicit switch conditions over generic caveats.

Examples:
- retain A while latency stays below an accepted threshold;
- switch to B if owner priority changes to offline resilience;
- defer only until a specific blocker is resolved;
- choose reversible A now and revisit after evidence Z.

## Information value boundary

Decision reconciliation may recommend EXPERIMENT_FIRST or DEFER but does not authorize evidence acquisition. If the authority selects new research, research-design owns the Evidence Plan.
