## 8. Consume Evidence Assessments without re-reviewing by default

Treat current Evidence Assessments as the normal epistemic input.

Do not reopen raw evidence or rerun review to rescue a desired outcome.

Bounded reassessment may be required only when:
- assessment subject/version mismatches the decision subject;
- assessment is stale for a materially time-sensitive decision;
- two assessments are logically incompatible enough to block synthesis;
- a visible provenance/authority defect exists.

Route epistemic reassessment back to `loom-foo-evidence-review` when needed.
