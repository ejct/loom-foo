> Preserved sections from private Loom-foo 0.35.1 SKILL.md. Referenced `references/...` paths are relative to the owning skill root. L1 activation and authority remain controlling.

## Boundary examples

- "Design a logo and identity for a Ukrainian/English brand" -> Identity Design if available; general Design when the task integrates unrelated disciplines or the specialist is unavailable.
- "Improve onboarding UX and show working states" -> Experience Design if available; otherwise general Design with honest prototype limitations.
- "Create an infographic from the attached dataset" -> Information Design for the graphic; Data for calculations and source evidence; never fabricate values.
- "Prepare a complete multi-format brand launch with campaign, UI, typographic samples and icon family" -> general Design owns integrated creative intent; specialists are optional methods, not obligatory subagents.
- "Prepare a brochure for this printer" -> general or Graphic Design + print constraints; a PDF draft is not verified PDF/X production.
- "Generate a simple cat picture" -> ordinary image generation, no compulsory design skill process.
- "Architect the loom-foo design skill family" -> `loom-foo-architecture-design` and `loom-foo-skill-engineering`, not this consumer design skill.
